# Candidate profile

Copy to `config/profile.md` and edit. `profile.md` is gitignored: it is your data, not the project's.

Keep this short. It is read on every evaluation, so length costs time on every role.

## Targeting

Lanes in priority order. A direct hit scores 4-5, adjacent scores 3, a mismatch scores 1-2.

1. **Primary lane** (e.g. Recruiting / Talent Acquisition / Sourcing)
2. **Secondary lane** (e.g. Recruiting Ops, Talent Operations)
3. **Third lane** (e.g. Customer Success / Client Operations)

## Hard filters, an automatic FAIL

- Location outside the countries you can work in
- Compensation stated below your floor
- Requires a licence, clearance or credential you do not hold
- Scope you do not want (for example people-management if you want IC roles)

## Compensation

- Floor: `$X`
- Target: `$Y`
- Any exceptions (role types where the floor does not apply)

## Location

- Where you live, and the fact that surrounding metro areas are your **home market**, not a relocation. Be explicit; a model will otherwise read a nearby suburb as a mismatch.
- Remote preference
- Cities you would move to

## Proof points

Three to six concrete things you have done, with real numbers. These are what a
match is judged against, so vague entries produce vague scores.

## Known gaps

Things you genuinely do not have. Stating them stops the evaluator inventing a
fit that is not there, and makes a FAIL explainable rather than mysterious.
